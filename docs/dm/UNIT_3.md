# DM UNIT 3 — Algebraic structures (Phase 2: generic checkers and groups)

## 1. Groups (implemented in Phase 2)

### Definitions
Semigroup (associative), monoid (semigroup + identity), group (monoid +
inverses). Subgroup: nonempty subset closed under the operation containing
identity and inverses. Cyclic group: generated by one element; generators of
Z_n are exactly the k with gcd(k,n)=1. Homomorphism: f(xy)=f(x)f(y).

### Theorem (Lagrange)
The order of a subgroup of a finite group divides the order of the group.
Verified exhaustively for (Z_n,+) subgroups <k> whose order is n/gcd(n,k).

### Use in Verity
- `GroupLaws` checkers validate the algebra demos in the DM Lab and CLI.
- The **alias-union monoid**: entity alias merging is defined over the
  AliasSet aggregate (union, empty identity) — a genuine commutative monoid.
  The brief explicitly forbids claiming a monoid without valid merge
  semantics; AliasSet provides them.
- Phase 3 reuses `MonoidLaws` for noisy-OR over [0,1] with identity 0.

### Worked example
(Z₆, +): group; generators {1,5}; subgroup <2> = {0,2,4} of order 3 (3 | 6 ✓).
(Z₆, ×) with identity 1 is NOT a group — honest failure (2,3,4 lack inverses).
f(x)=x+1 mod 6 is NOT a homomorphism: f(3)=4 but f(1)+f(2)=2+3=5.
